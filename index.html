Swoop Game Rules

Objective:

Be the first player to empty all cards from your Hand and your 8 Table Cards. The round ends immediately, and all other players tally penalty points.

1. Deal & Layout

Every player is dealt 19 cards:

  • 4 Mystery Cards: Dealt face-down on the table. Nobody knows what they are!
  • 4 Face-Up Table Cards: Placed directly on top of the 4 mystery cards.
  • 11 Hand Cards: Kept hidden in your hand.

2. Free Table & Mystery Plays at Any Time

You do not need to wait until your hand is empty to play your table cards!

  • Face-Up Table Cards: Can be played at any time on your turn. You can even combine a face-up table card with matching cards from your hand!
  • Mystery Cards: Once a face-up card is cleared, the mystery card beneath is exposed. You can click to blind-flip it onto the pile at any time. If it's equal or lower (or a Swoop), the play succeeds. If it busts (higher rank), you collect the center pile plus the mystery card into your hand!

3. Rank Hierarchy & Playing Rules

Card ranking goes: Ace (1, Lowest), 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, Jack, Queen, King (Highest).

The Golden Rule: You must play cards that are EQUAL TO OR LOWER in rank than the top card of the center play pile. Multiple cards of the same rank can be played together.

4. The SWOOP Special Powers!

  • 10s & Jokers: Any 10 or Joker is a SWOOP card! It can be played on ANY card at ANY time. It completely burns/clears the entire pile into the Swoop Bin, and gives that player an immediate bonus turn.
  • Four-of-a-Kind SWOOP: Whenever four matching cards of identical rank are consecutively completed on top of the pile, a SWOOP occurs! The pile clears, and the swooping player takes another turn!

5. Picking Up the Pile

If you cannot play an equal or lower card (and have no Swoop 10 or Joker), you must pick up the entire play pile and add all those cards into your hand!

6. Scoring

At the end of a round: Aces = 1 pt, 2-9 = face value, J/Q/K = 10 pts, 10s and Jokers = 50 penalty points. When a player accumulates the target limit (300 or 500 points), the game ends and the player with the lowest penalty score wins!
